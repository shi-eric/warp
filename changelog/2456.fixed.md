Reject NanoVDB index grids in `wp.Volume.load_from_nvdb()` whose leaf offsets or prefix sums map voxels past `Volume.get_voxel_count()` or the kernel limit of `2**31 - 1` indexable voxels. `wp.volume_sample_index()` and `wp.volume_lookup_index()` could otherwise lead to out-of-bounds accesses in per-voxel arrays. Grids that also assign indices to tile values or per-node statistics, which NanoVDB's grid builder can add, may exceed the voxel count and now fail to load.
